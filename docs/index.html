The Problem

Your body signals problems daily.
You're not listening.

Urine is the richest biological data stream your body produces — and you flush it without reading a single signal.

🚨

UTIs Strike Without Warning

Over 150 million UTI cases occur globally each year. Most people don't know they have one until the pain is unbearable — then it's an urgent care visit at 2am.

"I'd pay almost anything to not go to urgent care on a Sunday night." — r/UrinaryTractInfections
💉

Diabetes Monitoring is Invasive

530 million people live with diabetes worldwide. Current monitoring requires daily finger pricks or expensive CGMs. Non-invasive glucose indicators have been inaccessible — until now.

"I just want something passive that tells me when to call my doctor." — r/diabetes_t2
🏥

Kidney Disease Goes Undetected

1 in 7 adults has chronic kidney disease and most don't know it. Early detection via protein and creatinine levels can prevent dialysis — but lab tests cost $25–$80 each visit.

"My doctor said monitor closely but gave me no tool to actually do that." — r/kidneydisease
