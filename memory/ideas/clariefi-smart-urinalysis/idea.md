# Idea: Clariefi — Smart AI-Powered Urinalysis Device

## Status
active

## Slug
clariefi-smart-urinalysis

## One-Line Description
A hands-free, AI-powered home urinalysis device that continuously analyzes urine in real-time to track hydration, detect UTIs, monitor diabetes/glucose, and assess kidney health — paired with a mobile app delivering personalized health insights.

## Product Summary

**Device:** Toilet-integrated or standalone unit with passive funnel mechanism for hands-free urine collection. Uses replaceable disposable sensor cartridges ($30/pack) with self-cleaning, antimicrobial coating. Connects to mobile app via Bluetooth.

**Health Metrics Tracked:**
- Hydration levels (pH, specific gravity)
- UTI detection (nitrites, leukocytes)
- Diabetes monitoring (glucose, ketones)
- Kidney health (protein/albumin, creatinine)
- Metabolic health (ketones)

**Technology Stack:**
- Biosensors: pH sensor, refractometer, colorimetric/optical sensors, electrochemical glucose sensors, immunoassay sensors, enzyme-based detection
- Mobile app with Bluetooth connectivity and AI-driven health insights
- Cloud-based data storage (HIPAA/GDPR compliant)
- Real-time alerts for significant health changes

## Business Model
- Hardware (subscription model): $199–$299 one-time device purchase
- Monthly subscription: $10–$20/month for AI analytics & insights
- Disposable cartridges: $30/pack (ongoing consumable revenue)
- Premium one-time purchase: $399–$499, no subscription, AI included

## Target Segments
1. Health-conscious individuals (hydration tracking, keto diet users)
2. Chronic disease patients (diabetes, kidney disease, UTI-prone)
3. Elderly care & assisted living
4. B2B: hospitals, clinics, wellness centers, insurance providers

## Go-To-Market
- Pre-launch: Kickstarter crowdfunding
- Social: Reddit health groups, Facebook health communities
- Direct: Company website + Amazon
- B2B: Hospital/clinic partnerships, insurance provider deals

## Competitive Landscape (from founder research)
- **Withings U-Scan** ($499): In-toilet, tracks pH & ketones only, expensive, limited parameters
- **Vivoo**: Manual urine dipping, single-use strips, not real-time
- **Healthy.io**: Manual handling, AI smartphone analysis, no continuous monitoring
- **Inui Health**: Limited parameters, no smart device integration
- **Clarity UTI Test**: Single-function only (UTI)

**Claimed gap:** No fully automated, real-time, continuous home urinalysis solution exists

## Regulatory Context

- HIPAA & GDPR compliance required for health data
- Health Canada: Medical Device License (MDL) required
